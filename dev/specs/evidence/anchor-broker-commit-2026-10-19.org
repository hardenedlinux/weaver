* Lean composition and concrete assumptions

| Declaration | Checked consequence |
|-------------+---------------------|
| projection / base_reachable | Every wrapper event projects into the actual initialized preceding Weaver relation |
| reachable_invariant | Committed entries retain domain/configuration/body; actual outcomes have original qualified dispatch records |
| qualified_prefix_is_single_valued | Two registered qualified entries at one position are identical |
| qualified_burns_preserve_original_body | Qualified burns for one canonical key cannot disagree on original permit or writer epoch |
| one_canonical_grant_and_consumption | Grant and burn counts stay at most one through leadership/recovery |
| actual_application_requires_qualified_original_dispatch | Actual application has a registered committed original burn, exact key/permit and actual observed writer term/epoch |
| no_duplicate_dispatch_or_application | Each original key has at most one actual submission and one actual account application |
| original_shared_budget_conserved | Actual account amounts stay covered by original booked reservations and shared capacity |
| application_has_original_usage | Key, lineage and full original final usage remain bound |
| held_unknown_keeps_original_reservation | Election, lost responses and held permission do not release the original hold |
| root_term_monotone / actual_writer_epoch_monotone | Leadership term and actual writer epoch independently do not decrease |
| actual_barrier_blocks_held_permission / old_epoch_cannot_dispatch_after_reactivation | Physical closure and subsequent epoch mismatch reject old execution permission |
| change_leader_does_not_erase_original_execution | Election preserves registered history, held confirmations and actual submitted original IO |

RootOwner.commitValid is a qualified durable-publication premise. Its
currentReadValid and barrierBound predicates are indexed by the selected root,
configuration, term and head/target epoch. G.EvidenceOwner.barrierValid separately
requires actual no-new-submission isolation at the physical owner; matching fields
alone do not supply it. Qualified entries additionally belong to the registered
single committed prefix. This proves neither a quorum algorithm nor agreement of
arbitrary external signatures. Fixed configuration, correct prefix inheritance,
authentic original response ownership, all-alias isolation and actual original
account mutation/receipt atomicity require concrete owner evidence.

The actual writer stays in its previous coordinates during an anchor election
until physical isolation. The safe model deliberately permits the original
already confirmed operation, or its late original confirmation, to dispatch once
before that barrier. It prohibits new control-side consumption, replay from status
and any old dispatch after the actual barrier. A consumed-but-unsubmitted burn
cannot complete; a submitted original can finish while control admission remains
closed. Full-shard unknown retention can prevent activation forever.

The Lean commit entry retains the full permit; Quint records the determining
pre-commit root image and projects its full sealed permit for grant/burn entries.
The seal source request remains checked by the imported source action. These
encodings are documented correspondences, not a proven generated translation.
The effect in this receipt is internal account application, not provider byte
submission or external payment. Their original Policy/stream guards remain
separate required contracts.
