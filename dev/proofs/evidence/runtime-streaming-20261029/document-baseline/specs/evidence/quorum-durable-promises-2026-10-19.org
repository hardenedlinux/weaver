* Lean composition and unproved premises

[[file:../lean/QuorumDurablePromises.lean][The new Lean module]] proves protected-history extension for every permitted
step and finite run, historical witness retention, equal-position certificate
uniqueness across recovery, both membership quorum bridges, irrevocable
retirement, old-incarnation inequality and protected witnesses for every new
root publication. Its initialized product refines the preceding configuration
machine and inherits original burn/dispatch/account-write uniqueness, shared
budget conservation and retention of original unknown reservations.

[[file:quorum-promise-traces/QuorumDurablePromises.log][Printed axiom dependencies]] contain no =sorryAx= or added axioms. Some theorems
need no axioms; inherited results use only standard =propext=, =Quot.sound= and
=Classical.choice=. This is a conditional logical result: the original source
owner/store contracts, authenticated quorum family and full installation guards
remain inputs. The new component protects structural records. Native digest
encoding and signature binding of the complete source deciding image are not
proved by its list-prefix lemmas.

Quint uses six voters, four prefix slots, concrete old/joint/new majority rules
and integer operation choices. Lean uses arbitrary finite structural histories
and Nat-indexed voters over the existing initialized source model. There is no
extracted/refinement proof equating the two representations to native execution.
The sticky profile cannot replace an abandoned promise with a different term or
envelope. An election can therefore block forever; no liveness claim is made.

Native acceptance still requires atomic promise-before-sign under concurrency,
antirollback key/incarnation/retirement custody, fresh full-prefix recovery,
old-signer physical or mediated isolation, authenticated reconfiguration and
actual broker/account-writer FD isolation. Signer retirement is not evidence of
transport isolation. The original pre-barrier late-burn window and already
submitted IO completion rules remain intact. No arbitrary provider/payment
exactly-once or crash-tested native durability result is claimed.
