* Interpretation and limits

The journal proof preserves reachability of the actual arbitrary finite ledger
payload and derives recovered account conservation only from an authenticated
complete current image. Operation records are immutable across host rollback,
lost replies and lookups. The decision counter is audit ghost state; publication
does not consult it as a runtime authority guard. Freshness comes from the actual
independent canonical journal and its command/operation CAS contract.

The orthogonal range model starts after one range has been admitted, with its
epoch normalized to zero. ProtectedHandoff separately requires a real anchored
original claim, recovered state and actual-owner gate. The journal's empty initial
state is not silently treated as containing that claim. Request projection,
cryptographic input authentication and real transport refinement remain premises.

The normal post-cut drain witness passes safety while showing an old range can
be exposed after seal. A receiver fence can reject its business application
without preventing those wire bytes. The wire counter denotes bounded
application-range exposure events, not packet/TCP retransmission counts or exact
partial-write offsets. The single-range bound requires the qualified delegation
contract and cannot be generalized to arbitrary SDK behavior.

Co-rollback counterexamples retain specification/observational audit facts to
expose lost authority or repeated effects. Those ghosts are not a hidden runtime
oracle. If all freshness and owner/deduplication authority rolls back with the
host, local equality cannot establish safety. The dossier requires independently
qualified identity, durability, actual-writer mediation and receiver evidence.

Several rejection controls end in Quint's fail combinator and have no successor
s in their final trace entry. That is test semantics, not persistent state loss.
Passing negative tests mean the weaker design produced its expected violation;
those invalid actions remain excluded from normal step.

All twelve physical acceptance rows AR1-AR12 remain Pending. No VM rollback,
database fsync/crash, native SDK/kernel, actual receiver or wire-capture
qualification was executed. Simulator time below is not gateway performance.
