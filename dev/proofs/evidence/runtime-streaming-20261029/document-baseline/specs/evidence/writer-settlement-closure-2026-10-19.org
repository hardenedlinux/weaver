* Meaning and remaining acceptance gate

[[file:../../docs/architecture/23-writer-isolation-settlement-closure.org][The design dossier]] states exact reuse, physical premises and acceptance
requirements. The product separates evidence blobs, canonical ownership decisions,
local publication, message closure, final usage and settlement. It proves their
abstract joins, immutability, actual original-ledger projection and finite shared
reservation coverage. It does not establish crash-atomic storage, fsync semantics,
irrevocable FD/offload fencing, actual writer-inventory completeness, provider
finality, external payment idempotency, native ABI or deployed behavior.

The selected profile never reconstructs an old tail, preserves unknown holds,
permanently quarantines isolated uncertain streams and does not refund the original
fixed reservation. Over-envelope or conflicting usage blocks normal settlement;
a separate accounting/repair obligation is not implemented here. Physical failure
injection and source-to-storage/driver/provider refinement remain pending.
