oracles and physical acceptance obligations. Authenticated original-driver
quiescence and actual external isolation are trusted input events. Durable
completion and slot release share one abstract owner transaction; no concrete
multi-store atomicity, fsync, raw-FD revocation, source admission, Cedar execution,
provider final-usage authenticity or native deployed integration is established.

The proved profile keeps unknown occupancy and escrow, forbids adopting old
tails after driver restart, and permanently quarantines isolated uncertain
streams. It does not prove eventual delivery, refundable actual cost or safe
tail transfer. Physical failure injection remains pending; no runtime end-to-end
closure or high-performance measurement is claimed.
