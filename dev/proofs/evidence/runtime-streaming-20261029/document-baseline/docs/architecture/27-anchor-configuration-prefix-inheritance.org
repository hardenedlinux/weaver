* Decision and scope

Use one serialized configuration lineage and one authoritative prefix containing
both original data operations and configuration transitions. A replacement quorum
inherits the full deciding history, including original seals, issuance/burn
records, authentic results and unresolved obligations. It never initializes a
fresh financial or Policy domain. During joint configuration, neither the old
nor the new quorum can independently approve a root append.

This extends [[file:26-anchor-commit-broker-fencing.org][the fixed-membership commit/broker review]]. The selected conservative
profile requires old and new quorum certificates for both entering and finishing
joint configuration. New economic admission stays closed throughout the joint
phase and until fresh configuration-bound physical fencing and activation.
Original previously committed confirmations and submitted IO remain resolvable.
No timeout, failed migration or election rolls the configuration back to old-only.

[[file:../../specs/lean/AnchorReconfiguration.lean][The Lean extension]] composes with the actual initialized eleven-layer Weaver
model; [[file:../../specs/quint/AnchorReconfiguration.qnt][the Quint extension]] exercises concrete majority families, copied-prefix
faults, elections, late confirmations and actual account replay counterexamples.
These are formal semantic models, not extracted proofs of Scheme/Go/Rust or a
consensus engine. [[file:../../specs/evidence/anchor-reconfiguration-2026-10-19.org][The check receipt]] keeps that boundary explicit. [[file:diagrams/28-anchor-configuration-prefix.d2][Canonical D2]]
and [[file:diagrams/28-anchor-configuration-prefix.svg][its preview]] contain core design only.

The tracked external effect is the original internal account application. Its
execution never authorizes provider bytes or stream-tail reconstruction. Earlier
Policy/revocation, source-admission and transport-owner contracts remain required;
no external payment/provider exactly-once claim is made.

* Identity and full envelope

Keep root publication identity, configuration lineage, active membership, joint
transition, leadership term, committed position and actual writer epoch distinct.
A configuration change does not change canonical message, claim, account,
budget/history lineage, original usage, closure or application purpose. New
members and their credentials do not create new spending authority.

The concrete commit envelope binds the root, immutable membership descriptors,
current old/joint/new stage, transition revision, leadership term, one physical
committed position, complete original payload and determining prefix/source roots.
A joint envelope carries qualified old and new certificates. Replica installation,
barrier, current-read and activation receipts bind this same stage/revision and
exact original domain. Reusing an old signature or relabeling a barrier receipt
with the new membership descriptor is not valid evidence.

Membership IDs are immutable and never reused. The model chooses a serialized
successor lineage; returning to an earlier voter roster requires a new identity,
not restoration of its old publication context. There is one in-flight joint
transition, with no skipped or concurrent membership replacement. A native
configuration registry must authenticate descriptor-to-ID binding and reject
counter exhaustion, alias collisions and unauthorized member changes.

* Old, joint and new authority

| Stage / append | Required quorum authority | Required inheritance / execution condition |
|----------------+---------------------------+--------------------------------------------|
| Stable old data | Qualified old quorum | Full current determining prefix; original operation and Policy binding |
| Enter joint | Old quorum and replacement quorum | Both acknowledge the exact prior prefix and deciding image before committing transition |
| Joint result or fence data | Old quorum and new quorum | Same joint stage/revision; original result or actual owner fence evidence |
| Joint new economic admission | Closed | No seal/grant/burn merely because either quorum is available |
| Finish joint | Old quorum and new quorum | Exact prefix now includes joint entry and any intervening data/results/fences |
| Stable new data | Qualified new quorum | Full inherited prefix; fresh new-stage activation before new economic admission |

Old data versus enter-joint intersect in the old quorum system; joint versus
stable-new intersect in the new quorum system. Old and new voter sets themselves
may be disjoint. This does not justify independently publishing under both sets:
the joint bridge and prefix transfer must be committed before new-only authority
is usable. Each quorum system's intersection property is an explicit Lean family
contract; Quint checks concrete two-of-three families and disjoint roster examples.
Intersection supplies a common witness, not agreement by itself. Durable
non-equivocation at that witness and committed-prefix election/recovery rules
remain qualified store-owner obligations. The model does not prove a consensus
voting/election algorithm from these facts.

The selected dual-quorum enter rule is stricter than protocols that enter joint
state under old-only commitment with additional election rules. Those protocols
are not qualified by this model. This profile can block indefinitely if the
replacement cannot inherit the prefix or either required quorum is unavailable.
It does not fall back to a cached configuration or a new empty root.

* Complete deciding image and installation

Install a candidate as a learner before treating it as an acknowledging member.
A qualified installation receipt authenticates the exact committed data/control
prefix and original determining state at the transition cut. In the checked
profile, every signer used by the selected certificate has installed that image.
A certificate may choose another valid quorum when a member is unavailable; it
cannot include a signer whose installation is missing or stale.

| Image component | Required purpose |
|-----------------+------------------|
| Complete mixed data/control prefix | Preserve the committed configuration stage and every earlier original operation; matching a maximum index is insufficient |
| Seals, issuance tombstones, burns and authentic results | Preserve original spent-state even with a missing reply or missing account projection |
| Original source authority and producer roots | Preserve claims, original reservations, budget/history domains, final usage and confirmation identity |
| Configuration lineage / stage / revision | Prevent old-only publication or old receipt acceptance as current new authority |
| Actual owner reference and unresolved obligations | Resolve original IO conservatively; never deserialize an execution continuation |

The Lean image carries the full prefix, consumption-root journal and the prior
source recovery projection with account observation counters erased. It excludes
live grant/burn response continuations, execution capabilities and the new
wrapper's dispatch counters. Source model values are semantic determining records,
not runnable descriptors or a native snapshot serialization recipe.

Quint checks the mixed prefix and full consumption-root image, including complete
sealed permits with original usage/history binding. It preserves the imported
source relation across every control transition rather than duplicating the
entire source image in each replica fixture. Lean checks the fuller source-image
installation guard. This is a documented correspondence and scope difference;
there is no automated Quint/Lean translation or native copy verifier.

The exact image is a qualified available cut, not access to unseen semantic
execution facts. If required actual-owner deciding evidence cannot be materialized,
installation/finalization is unavailable. Preserve uncertainty; never fill an
absent application receipt with a claim that no effect occurred.

Installation is not admission. It neither reissues a capability nor enqueues an
unknown operation. A newer prefix after any append requires fresh installation
for the next selected certificate in this conservative model. Real incremental
replication can replace full-copy work only with a qualified complete-prefix
receipt; it cannot weaken deciding-record coverage.

* Ordering, response loss and old acknowledgements

1. Authenticate the proposed membership change as an actual Governance operation.
   Stage the exact old/new descriptor pair under the existing root and lineage.
2. Install the current deciding prefix on the selected old and new signers.
   Commit enter-joint once with both certificates; close new economic admission.
   A lost response resolves this same committed control record and stage.
3. Preserve the complete joint state across elections. Any root result/fence
   append while joint requires both quorums and current-prefix evidence. A stale
   old-scope proposal cannot commit even if its data-only index still matches.
4. Before finish-joint, install the updated full prefix, including enter-joint
   and all intervening appends. Commit finish-joint under both quorums. Retire
   old-only publication authority without erasing historical old certificates.
5. Obtain actual writer isolation, commit a fence under the current configuration,
   resolve unknown burns and read the complete current prefix/source image.
   Activate only stable-new with matching stage/revision, term and actual writer
   epoch. A prior joint-stage barrier/fence/read cannot be relabeled as new-stage
   activation evidence. If fencing was deferred until stable-new, one fresh fence
   can suffice; fencing performed in joint does not remove the final-stage join.

A genuinely committed old record remains historical evidence after reconfiguration.
Its original live grant/burn response can still arrive before the actual writer
barrier, and the old broker may submit that original operation once. An old-only
acknowledgement for an uncommitted or newly proposed entry is not such evidence.
Membership commitment does not instantly isolate old FD/callback/offload aliases.
Physical isolation ends new old-writer submissions; already submitted original IO
may finish later and supply its exact authentic result.

An old result can be archived under the current quorum rules without changing
its original operation identity. It cannot produce a fresh budget envelope or
another application. Missing original burn/application results remain unknown;
finalization may preserve those obligations, but full-shard execution activation
cannot open until the existing conservative recovery condition is met.

| Interleaving | Required result |
|--------------+-----------------|
| Old data commits before enter-joint | Transition inherits it; late original confirmation can resolve the same record |
| Enter-joint commits before old data proposal | Reject stale scope/revision/physical-head proposal; no old-only append |
| Enter/finish commit response lost | Resolve the exact current prefix/stage; no repeated control effect or new membership identity |
| New leader appears during joint | Inherit joint stage and both-quorum rule; never reset to stable old |
| Replica has correct data position but omits control or spent record | Installation fails exact determining-image comparison |
| Old and new quorums coexist but only one can respond | No joint commit; unknown original obligations remain held |
| Finish-joint races old barrier acknowledgement | Old stage/revision receipt cannot activate stable new |
| Another election follows a barrier | Rebind qualified actual owner evidence to current term/stage; old callback metadata cannot substitute |
| Old burn reply arrives before actual barrier | Only original committed once-only IO may enter old writer gate |
| Old burn reply arrives after actual barrier | No new submission; historical status/result resolution only |
| New members start from an empty application ledger | Reject missing inherited image; excluded fork mutant applies the same account effect twice |

* Formal composition without a runtime compatibility interface

The prior AnchorBrokerCommit entry uses a fixed configuration field. The new
proof retains it only as a stable logical projection namespace, while the outer
envelope carries the active membership stage/revision and one physical mixed
prefix. Erasure deletes membership-control records and projects data entries to
the earlier semantic relation. Its data-only indices are proof bookkeeping;
they are not a second production log, public API or current-membership selector.

Native admission must validate the full new envelope. Accepting the prior bare
entry or fixed-field certificate alone would bypass the new quorum and transfer
guards. No runtime compatibility layer or dependency migration is implemented.
This preserves prior theorem reuse without claiming that a fixed-membership
certificate already proves dynamic membership safety.

| Event | Quint action | Lean correspondence |
|-------+--------------+---------------------|
| Install exact deciding image | install / installAll | install with actual imageValid plus exact image equality |
| Data/root commit | commit | Actual prior root event plus current membership certificate, copied prefix, economic/fence guards |
| Enter joint / finish joint | enterJoint / finishJoint | Append control record, advance membership revision, preserve base authority and close admission |
| Election / original execution | changeLeader / acknowledge / dispatch / complete | core projects to prior event; control prefix unchanged |
| Configuration-bound actual barrier | acceptBarrier / isolateWriter | isolate with actual owner predicate and full scope/revision/term/epoch binding |
| Current read / activate | readCurrent / activate | Full mixed-prefix cut plus prior actual writer activation and stable-stage guards |

Lean proves append-only mixed prefixes, historical quorum qualification for every
committed record, embedding of every prior committed data
entry, monotone configuration identities/revisions, closed joint economic
admission, adjacent quorum intersections, exact installed-image requirements and
preservation of original consumption/dispatch/application counts, shared budget
coverage and unknown reservations. It starts from the actual initialized prior
product and quantifies over arbitrary finite manifests and finite traces along
the serialized configuration lineage, under immutable quorum-family contracts.

These proofs remain conditional on authentic durable publication and installation,
qualified current reads, actual all-alias barriers and actual account effect/
receipt atomicity. Prefix invariants do not prove arbitrary external certificate
agreement. The new quorum layer refines admission to the existing abstract single
prefix; it does not prove that a storage engine implements that prefix or its
membership transfer correctly. The retained negative traces expose those failed
premises rather than converting them into success assumptions.

Scheme owns original identity, claim/history/budget semantics and Governance
requirements. Cedar constrains the actual membership-change and original effect
requests against authenticated determining state. Go owns cloud-native discovery,
Kubernetes/workload identity, learner orchestration and reconciliation of exact
committed stages; ConfigMap/lease changes are proposals, not commit authority.
Rust validates complete membership envelopes, installation/prefix joins and the
actual broker barrier/activation contract. Qualified storage/effect owners supply
the physical facts. No language implementation is changed by this review.

* Native acceptance and performance boundary

Exercise disjoint rosters, old-only/new-only partitions, learner promotion before
copy completion, old snapshots omitting control entries or burns, old leader
restoration, a quorum witness restored from a pre-acknowledgement snapshot,
lost enter/finish acknowledgements, elections at each transition,
old FD/callback/buffer recovery and fence/read/finish races. Require identical
original consumption history and booked obligations before and after transfer.
Validate late original receipts against their historical prefix while rejecting
fresh old-only appends. Never repair an unknown prefix by starting a new domain.

Reconfiguration is a slow control path but may pause hot economic admission.
Full copied-image guards here are a conservative semantic cut, not a measured
replication design. Incremental replication, batching and quorum selection need
qualified equivalent receipts before making performance claims. Original shared
budget/history domains cannot be split to create capacity. No benchmark, native
consensus acceptance or gateway implementation is claimed; dependency pins stay
unchanged.
