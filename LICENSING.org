#+language: en

* Dual license

Weaver's original contributions are offered under
=LGPL-2.1-or-later OR Apache-2.0=. A recipient may choose either license.
The OR operator expresses a choice; it does not require both licenses at once.

Copyright: 2026 hardenedlinux Linux Team and Contributors.

The complete license texts are retained verbatim:

- [[file:LICENSES/LGPL-2.1-or-later.txt][GNU Lesser General Public License 2.1 or later]]
- [[file:LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt][Apache License 2.0]]

* Machine-readable metadata

[[file:REUSE.toml][REUSE.toml]] associates SPDX copyright and license identifiers
with Weaver-authored code, configuration, tests and design sources. Its closest
precedence preserves individual file notices. Scheme source headers carry the
same identifiers. The Cargo workspace supplies the expression to every Rust
member; the renderer package declares the same expression. Go source is covered
by the repository notice and SPDX annotations.

* Retained third-party and historical material

The project choice applies to Weaver's original contributions. Dependency
licenses, embedded fonts and external brand assets retain their own terms;
no repository annotation grants new rights to those components. Generated SVGs
and archived evidence are not blanket-relicensed by REUSE.toml. Historical
snapshots retain their original notices and provenance. This scope is not a
claim that every archived or generated file has a complete REUSE annotation.

The existing [[file:docs/architecture/diagrams/assets/icon-sources.org][brand asset provenance]]
identifies the Go, Rust and Kubernetes marks. Fonts embedded by the upstream
render Library retain that Library's font-license notices and upstream font
attribution. Dependencies and marks are not relicensed as Weaver source.

The canonical identifiers and annotation format follow
[[https://spdx.org/licenses/LGPL-2.1-or-later.html][SPDX LGPL-2.1-or-later]],
[[https://spdx.org/licenses/Apache-2.0.html][SPDX Apache-2.0]] and the
[[https://reuse.software/spec-3.3/][REUSE specification]].
