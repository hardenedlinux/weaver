#+TITLE: Policy C ABI Preflight Benchmark
#+LANGUAGE: en

* Workload
Validate the same fixed typed Cedar Policy/schema fixture used by the Policy
package. Thirty samples retain the existing warmup and measurement settings.
The document-and-aggregate workload validates two complete policy sets followed
by their newline-separated aggregate under the same schema. It measures source
preparation work, not an extra check in the per-message execution path.

* Measurement boundary
This measures the safe byte-oriented preflight used by the ABI. It excludes
Scheme conversion, dynamic loading, gRPC, ledger CAS and final provider sends.
It is not a Gateway throughput or foreign-memory safety claim.

* Entrypoints
=cargo bench -p weaver-policy-ffi --bench gateway --locked --offline=.
The Go development runner also inventories and collects this workspace member.
