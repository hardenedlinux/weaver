* SysML decision before measurement

The existing =ControlActivation.GovernancePolicyProjection= owns this refinement
of [[file:59.70-borrowed-validation-dispatch.org][the borrowed-validation review]]. Runtime authority and measurement quality
remain separate. The current task explicitly permits direct diagnostic retesting
on the shared Host; a busy or battery-powered Host does not block those runs.
It also does not become controlled evidence by permission or by a favorable ratio.
Do not stop unrelated work, change power settings, shrink worker counts or wait
for repeated favorable samples.

* Predeclared evidence grades

| Grade | Conditions and permitted conclusion |
|-------+-------------------------------------|
| Shared-Host diagnostic | Record all observable conditions and missing fields; compare only descriptive matching samples; no default/runtime promotion |
| Controlled profile | Attested exclusive window plus stable physical/available CPU, power and thermal profile; AC and low-power mode off; 30-second preflight with idle >=90%, load1 <=0.5 times logical CPUs, normal memory pressure and zero swap-out growth; repeat before/after each leg |
| Intrinsic native CPU or device attribution | Additionally requires thread-CPU/scheduler and process-scoped kernel I/O evidence; wall-clock gets or FULL completion alone do not establish it |

An operator reservation label is an attestation reference, not independently
verified exclusivity. Missing CPU/power/thermal observations cannot count as a
controlled pass. A profile with dynamic frequency must disclose that fact and
retain stable native sentinels; no pure instruction-cost claim follows. A Linux
runner is another profile, not a substitute for Darwin regression acceptance.

* Fixed comparison and acceptance

Use actual available CPUs with a four-worker minimum for runtime/write/shared
budgets and reusable capacity; record physical/logical/available counts separately.
Keep the existing blocking budget, FULL, real bounded body/CID verification,
strict typed decoding and enrolled-authority checks,
original claim/reservation/history identities, zero retries and 30-second per-case
collection bound. Preparation/compilation is outside timing; accepted work drains
and fresh owners recover every original operation. Reporting/monitoring overhead
and shared-Host cache effects remain disclosed.

Freeze original O, ready-only A and Condvar-plus-ready AV binary identities with
the same workload/compiler/SDK/profile. Admission uses fixed O/A/A/O; validator
uses fixed A/AV/AV/A. The latter isolates validator replacement conditional on
ready admission; comparing AV directly to O would confound two changes. Preserve
all WAL/fresh/reuse rounds and hot/independent domains. Match treatment leg 2 to
reference leg 1 and treatment leg 3 to reference leg 4. Retain every operation,
failure and slow case. Combined production promotion would additionally require
a direct AV/O confirmation; conditional medians cannot simply be multiplied.
Do not treat operations within a leg as independent statistical replications.

Safety is a hard gate regardless of Host grade: unexpected failure/Unknown/refusal
or inexact recovery rejects a clean-run performance pass. Native once-only and
clock invariants stay active. Borrowed callback stays on its caller through the
native completion contract; rejection/panic/cancellation must retain original
unknown exposure and accepted-work cleanup. Historical replay creates no permit.
Fault-injection Unknown outcomes retain their existing expected interpretation;
they are not ordinary clean-run throughput failures.

For controlled engineering acceptance, each declared mode/domain stratum needs
no paired TPS ratio <0.95 and no paired P95/P99 >1.05 baseline. Predeclare
hot/reuse and independent/reuse as primary strata: median paired successful TPS
>=1.10 overall and >=1.05 in each of three fixed ABBA blocks. Diagnostic retesting
uses one block per candidate; it cannot qualify that controlled replication gate.
Boundaries are inclusive, fixed before this
retest, and are engineering thresholds rather than a confidence theorem. Require
native-control get and FULL-post-body mean max/min <=1.10 within each compared
profile/variant; larger drift makes causal attribution inconclusive. Unchanged
native-path controls separate common Host/context drift from dispatcher changes,
but cannot identify a kernel or MVCC routine without direct evidence.

The diagnostic retest remains useful even when these quality conditions fail;
it cannot waive safety, erase regressions or admit a candidate into production.
SPEC semantics and existing Lean/Quint responsibilities remain unchanged.

* Source-currentness before the retest

Both candidates are reconstructed at MRR PR 12 base =895a06c3= in separate
temporary worktrees, keeping manifest/lockfile identity. The initial shared
target attempt reused a stale leaf: the Condvar unit-test list lacked its new
close-order test. That attempt is provenance-invalid, not candidate acceptance.
Before switching source, clean only the backend package or use an isolated
target directory, record the actual compile path and candidate-specific test
witness, then freeze and hash the executable. Byte-identical dependency artifacts
may be reused; an unchecked cached backend result may not. No compilation/test
overlaps a timed leg. Candidates remain unmerged research variants.

* Direct retest results

MRR Data [[https://github.com/tao3k/mrr-data/pull/12][PR 12]] commit =87900243= owns the
[[https://github.com/tao3k/mrr-data/blob/87900243/docs/benchmarks/shared-backend-host-envelope.org][complete sample tables, source-currentness record, native controls and acceptance criteria]].
Weaver consumes this record instead of duplicating the MRR benchmark or backend.

The explicitly requested shared-Host retest completes eight full-path legs and
three native controls: 171 cases, 131,328 commits and exact original-operation
recoveries. No unexpected failure, refusal or Unknown occurs. All 11 processes
exit naturally and their executable hashes remain unchanged. Frozen artifacts
are independently rebuilt from current base plus candidate source; source/pin
identity and native safety are not waived with Host quality.

Raw values determine medians; displayed ratios are rounded. No candidate passes
the full nonregression requirements. The validator primary hot number is a
conditional diagnostic observation, not a general twofold speedup or proof of
combined AV/O benefit.

The same A executable's validator-reference hot reads change from
136.55--149.04 microseconds in leg 1 to 309.86--419.88 in leg 4; bridge residual
changes from 10.18--12.46 to 131.43--311.90. The late reference makes the second
half of AV/A look much larger. In the earlier lower-latency pair, median bridge
residual is 10.66 versus 8.50 microseconds, a descriptive 2.16-microsecond saving;
unchanged native stages vary too. Do not assign all observed changes to either
the protocol or Host CPU contention alone.

Native controls bypass both dispatch and ProfilePort rendezvous in their timed
loops. Original reuse gets and FULL-post-body max/min are 2.223 and 4.252;
AV reuse is 1.108 and 1.227. The 10-percent sentinel gate is exceeded. AV control
reuse/WAL get ratios 1.157, 1.063, 1.207 retain a modest lower-layer read-cost
tradeoff; query/materialization and driver wait still need separate attribution.
There is no new proof of pure native CPU cost, kernel fsync time or a fixed
intrinsic MVCC penalty.

Every leg-boundary observation reports 12 physical/logical CPUs, normal memory
pressure and AC power; all executables use 12 runtime/write/shared workers,
reuse capacity 12 and 16 blocking threads. Load1 ranges 16.60--45.68. The initial
battery discovery sample is not the power state of the timed retest. Exclusive
ownership, idle/thermal trajectory and cumulative swap-out remain unestablished;
the result is diagnostic, as requested, rather than a blocked experiment or a
controlled-profile pass.

Forced candidate safety passes 82 tests for A and 83 for AV, including real
borrowed guard, typed rejection, panic, cancellation and restart checks. AV's
new close-order witness actually runs after the stale leaf is removed. Both
candidates and the original runtime pass strict Clippy; original standard reuse
safety passes 82. Original operation/authority/claim/history and typed held=4
remain bound. Existing SPEC and Lean/Quint responsibilities remain unchanged.

Neither candidate is promoted, and no production path or dependency pin changes.
Keep every sample and provenance-invalid receipt separate. Subsequent native
attribution should distinguish query preparation, stepping/materialization and
driver wait before adding caches or weakening borrowed semantics. Benchmark
readiness never supplies execution authority or releases unknown exposure.
