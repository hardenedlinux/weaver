* Observed failure
The original native fixture validates the same bounded Contract against a POO
object and actual JSON decoding, with eleven assertions plus duplicate-key
rejection. Its completion marker is not process termination. The original
fifteen-second acceptance budget stays unchanged for the entire process.

Retained no-force-exit diagnostic runs naturally returned zero after the budget.
Late zero exit remains a failed gate. A longer post-failure observation window
is diagnostic only; it does not increase the acceptance budget or qualify a
late run. No diagnostic process was forced to return success or skip cleanup.

* Current process samples
Sampling inside the filesystem sandbox was refused. A narrowly scoped sample of
the verification's own process outside that sandbox succeeded; errors and
sample commands are retained. Do not infer a stack from a missing sample file.

The load-phase sample contains 90 of 96 main-thread samples in
=___dynamic_load -> dlopen= and 89 in =notifyDebuggerLoad=. The sample taken
immediately after completion contains 96 of 96 in
=___cleanup_dyn_module -> dlclose= and 95 in =notifyDebuggerUnload=, with
synchronous remote-notification waits. Thus both image loading and proper
unloading contribute to the wall-clock failure. This is observed Darwin loader
latency amplification, not evidence that the assertions failed or that the
process can never finish.

The post-check diagnostic observed completion at 10.874 seconds and natural
exit at 18.653 seconds. Another diagnostic observed completion at 20.840 seconds
and exit at 27.829 seconds. Sampling and concurrent compilation can perturb
these diagnostic timings; they are not a matched performance benchmark. The
uninstrumented historical timeout independently establishes the failed gate.
The old unload-order experiment from another task is not reused as a fix.

* Repair boundary
The bounded native check should execute a coherent compiled source closure, with
its macro/package work done during building. Repeatedly loading separate Scheme
images while interpreting the fixture adds loader/notification work to both
startup and cleanup. A compiled AOT entry links the Scheme modules into the
verification image and retains ordinary platform libraries and actual cleanup.
This is a verifier build/entry repair, not a modification to Gambit's unload loop
or an assurance that arbitrary dynamic gxi workloads now meet the deadline.

No =___DONT_UNLOAD_DYN_CODE=, retained leaked dlopen handles, artificial keepalive,
forced successful exit or relaxed acceptance clock is introduced. A dynamically
loaded cleanup canary tests that normal dynamic destruction still occurs.
