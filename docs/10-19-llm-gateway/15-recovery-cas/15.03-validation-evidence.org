* Composition proof and explicit premises

=publication_projection= maps each consuming transition to an actual category-14
=advance= and every background source mutation to an actual prior step with no
new protected effect. The prior execution projection then preserves original
claim uniqueness, profile interpretation, account domains, response mode and
allowance. Unknown child usage prevents a root settlement change.

The new decision audit proves each recorded request equals the actual accepted
cut and actual Policy was Allow. =decision_ids_remain_unique= proves pairwise
distinct durable decision IDs. Confirmations are members of the durable decision
log and cannot change execution or append a new decision. Final freshness lemmas
exclude old Policy generation, owner fence and execution revision.

This is a conditional state-machine composition proof. Authority values are
logically serialized, while native durable authenticity, independent non-rollback
recovery, effective authority publication and =owner.commitValid= are explicit
owner contracts. The generation represents the external Policy/history domain;
it does not authenticate arbitrary caller domain names. The model does not prove
consensus, cryptography, a native distributed transaction, real-time lease expiry,
remote execution, a full asynchronous range/account product or liveness.

In particular, a read callback cannot enforce the owner premise by itself. A
separately effective external change that can bypass the final owner's generation
would be a native refinement failure. Such a deployment is not admitted by the
proof. Publication of permission changes and consumption must share a qualified
serialization point or a protocol excluding conflicting commits.

* Native acceptance and performance gates

Inject a Policy/history update after each individual read and immediately before
CAS. Test Allow/Deny/Allow with the same visible Boolean but newer generation.
Race final consumption against owner restart, effective revocation, independent
ledger writes and profile-cache recovery. Retain real old issued permits and
queued callbacks. Lose commit replies, read exact decisions after restart and
reject operation-ID aliases. Do not retry based on stale local absence.

Verify effective Policy/history acknowledgements cannot precede their publication
to the final serialization authority, or qualify the selected participant protocol.
Retain unknown budget until attributable original-slot usage and settlement.
Collect source-bound durable receipts and matched performance controls. Formal
receipts are sealed by [[file:../../../dev/proofs/evidence/llm-recovery-cas/SHA256SUMS][SHA256SUMS]]; previous receipts remain unchanged.

* Local validation result

The local runner checks 12 Lean modules and 19 printed declarations in the new
module, with no =sorryAx=. Quint checks 40 scenario controls: 32 guarded cases and
eight negative controls with exported traces. Seven initializers each receive
ten samples of at most 30 post-initializer steps, seed 173. These 70 samples are
bounded simulation, not exhaustive temporal verification. The category-14
regression runs 43 controls.

The 12 correspondence rows resolve to actual passing controls, printed Lean
statements and core D2 nodes. Independent trace decoding checks observable faults,
including actual prior issuance of an old permit, a mixed read across effective
denial, and a late confirmation of an already committed original decision.
Native serialization and durable commit remain separate acceptance obligations.
