* Composition and proof premises

=cas_projection= maps owner publication to the actual =policyChange= transition,
background source steps to their actual transitions with unchanged Policy state,
and restart to the actual prior owner/source restart path. Readable projection
loss/recovery and producer acknowledgement preserve the execution authority.
Original claim uniqueness, profile interpretation, account domains, allowance
and unknown-usage settlement constraints follow from this projection.

The new proofs establish generation/log alignment, effective-value/tip alignment,
exact staged provenance, pairwise unique published change IDs, inherited committed
prefixes, and an inductive exact-parent chain for arbitrary finite publications.
Every acknowledgement names a committed publication. Historical confirmation
cannot assign current authority, and final CAS still rejects old generation/fence.
The worker check on owner publication is separate from the historical record's
continued validity as data.

Durable producer approval, current actual-operation publication authorization,
exact source identity, durable publication/acknowledgement
receipts, authenticated owner recovery and non-rollback source state remain
explicit owner contracts. The effect/content commitments are typed identities;
cryptographic hashing and payload interpretation need native qualification.
Logical serialization is required between publication and final consumption; the
proof does not implement distributed consensus, an outbox, leases or a multi-store
transaction. Whole-authority rollback violates the stated deployment premise.
The bounded Quint fixture is not exhaustive verification or a liveness proof.

for old-permit cases, actual newer committed denial and attributable historical
confirmation. An invalid placeholder or forged permit is insufficient evidence.

* Implementation acceptance still required

Lose responses after stage, owner append and producer acknowledgement. Restore
producer intent, owner visibility and acknowledgement projections independently.
Keep queued old callbacks and publisher handles alive across owner restart. Verify
exact stable-ID lookup and full payload equality; reject stale parents and stale
publishers rather than rebasing. Delay the old Allow acknowledgement until a newer
Deny has committed, and demonstrate that both old and newly reconstructed cached
permissions fail the final CAS.

Verify owner recovery checks the exact current log and ledger namespace/prefix,
not just local counters. Exercise generation exhaustion with fail-closed behavior.
Check that pending status is never presented as effective and that owner commit
invalidates old permits before producer confirmation. Preserve original unknown
holds until qualified original-slot usage and settlement. Native durability,
transport and performance acceptance remain separate from these model receipts.

* Local validation result

The local runner completed 24 command routes with exit zero: 13 Lean modules,
Quint typechecking, 45 scenario controls (37 guarded cases and eight negative
controls), focused exported counterexamples, seven sampling routes and 40
category-15 regression controls. Each sampling route used ten samples and at
most 30 post-initializer steps with seed 179. The 70 samples are bounded
simulation, not exhaustive temporal verification.

The new Lean module checked 19 printed declarations without =sorryAx=, including
the inductive exact-parent chain and composition into the prior final CAS model.
Twelve correspondence rows resolve to actual passing Quint controls, printed
Lean declarations and core D2 nodes. Independent trace decoding checks concrete
violations, including earlier issuance of the old permit, the newer durable Deny
and acknowledgement of the earlier Allow. Native storage/serialization and
deployment acceptance remain separate obligations.
