* Composition and proof premises

=continuation_projection= maps authoritative alias admission to an actual
category-17 =bindAlias= and every source mutation to its actual prior transition.
Bootstrap cannot publish, choose a winner, consume, settle or admit an unregistered
alias around the new gate. Original registration requires actual staged origin and
exact source binding, rather than a raw protocol locator.

The registry invariant binds the single committed registration to the admitted
origin/original, every authoritative alias to that exact registration and normalized
subject, and all alias keys pairwise distinctly. Registration is never replaced.
Whenever registration exists, the actual original remains staged. Resolution and
projection restoration cannot assign execution state. Final-use gates reject old
registry revision/fence; the full source cut and actual source transitions preserve
original claim, profile, domains, allowance, winner and unknown-usage settlement.

The admitted =expected= key and =owner.originValid= are source-issued origin
contracts. The proof does not derive semantic intent continuity from prompts or
wire IDs. Native enrollment must enforce origin uniqueness and authentic complete
namespaces. Canonical family classification must agree with that enrollment;
continuity, durable registration/alias receipts, current reads, recovery and actual
operation binding are explicit owner predicates. Treating those predicates as
caller-provided labels would invalidate the native refinement.

The model covers one origin and arbitrary finite alias lists. Global multi-origin
allocation, delegated-principal migration, cryptographic proofs, native storage
consensus, wire parsers/authentication, real-time leases, remote exactly-once and
liveness are not proved. Whole-authority rollback violates the non-rollback
premise. Native fast-path optimizations need equivalent scoped currentness laws.

* Implementation acceptance

Lose replies after origin/alias CAS; query exact authoritative keys and full bodies.
Race concurrent registration/binding with identity/source restart, Policy/history
changes and actual consumption. Restore origin, alias, pending, winner and source
ledger projections independently. Keep old callbacks and issued resolution receipts
alive. Reject duplicate/rebound keys, foreign tenant/principal/issuer/profile and
stale incarnation; require authenticated continuity for new MCP/A2A/ACP locators.

Verify fresh valid resolutions cannot reset original spent claims or bypass original
history denial. Preserve unknown holds and the original pending/winner through all
resume paths. Capture native source-bound current-prefix receipts and separate
wire-interoperability tests. These acceptance obligations are not satisfied merely
by passing this abstract model.

* Local validation result

The runner completed 26 command routes: 15 Lean modules, Quint typechecking,
49 scenario controls (41 guarded cases and eight negative controls), focused
exported counterexamples, seven sampling routes and 42 category-17 regression
controls. Each sampling route used ten samples and at most 30 post-initializer
steps with seed 191. These 70 samples are bounded simulation, not exhaustive
temporal verification.

The new Lean module checked 21 printed declarations without =sorryAx=. Twelve
four-artifact rows resolve to passing Quint controls, printed Lean statements
and core D2 nodes. Three fresh-resolution controls first assert that the exact
issued receipt passes current identity admission, then verify that original
spent-state or history denial rejects the actual operation. Independent trace
decoding checks the eight faults and actual prior old-resolution issuance.

Source review freshly verifies 33 pinned blobs across POO Flow, Cedar, ASP and
agentgateway, plus the three selected official protocol references. Native
multi-origin CAS, authenticated recovery, task-to-child mapping and wire
conformance remain separate acceptance obligations.
